NilAway ↗ Go nil-panic detection

[Learn More]

NilAway is a static analysis tool that detects potential crashes caused by accessing nil values in Go. I co-led its development and rollout across 70 million lines of Go, detecting 500+ potential nil panics monthly, and built an agentic system to propose and validate fixes that helped reduce reported nil panics by 80%+ in six months in one of Uber's largest organizations.With Lazaro Clapp, Julia Turcotti, and Sonal Mahajan.

RPCShield ↗ Microservice reliability

[Learn More]

I led RPCShield's inbound and outbound dependency analysis and infrastructure, identifying 1,000+ cross-tier fail-close violations in one year. This exposed cascading-failure risks and supported more efficient CPU allocation. I also expanded RPCShield's scope to power cross-organizational initiatives such as finer-grained service criticality classification.With Milind Chabbi, Sonal Mahajan, Yufan Xu, and many others at Uber.

Last Diff Analyzer ↗ Automated code review
