# Organization logos

These files are stored locally to avoid depending on external image hosts at runtime.
The artwork retains its original colors and proportions.

- `uber.svg`: [Uber wordmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Uber_logo_2018.svg), downloaded from https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/58/Uber_logo_2018.svg.
- `penn-state.svg`: [Penn State institutional mark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Pennsylvania_State_University_logo.svg), downloaded from https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/6/6b/Pennsylvania_State_University_logo.svg. See [Penn State's brand guide](https://brand.psu.edu/visual-identity-standards/university-mark-and-shield).
- `hust.svg`: [Huazhong University of Science and Technology logo](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Huazhong_University_of_Science_%26_Technology_logo.svg), downloaded from https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/zh/a/ab/Huazhong_University_of_Science_%26_Technology_logo.svg.

The logos are trademarks of their respective organizations, used here to identify employment and education affiliations.
